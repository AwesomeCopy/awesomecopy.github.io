AwesomeCopy

AwesomeCopy is a menu bar clipboard manager that captures everything you copy—text, images, file paths—with effortless speed and privacy. Browse up to 100 items instantly, pin favorites, and reuse them seamlessly across Mac, iPhone, and iPad via iCloud sync.

Lightning-Fast History

Access your clipboard history with one click. Copy on first selection, drag text into fields, or use Command+Delete to remove items. Time-based limits keep it lean and relevant.

Rich Media Support

Preview links, display audio album art, play audio in-app, and handle ICNS files. Original file types are preserved, with text-to-speech for quick reviews.

Seamless Sync

iCloud keeps your clipboard in sync across devices. Copy on iOS, paste on Mac—or vice versa. Deletions and category filters sync reliably, with a redesigned iOS keyboard.

Customization Everywhere

New Crayon Box theme, resizable edit windows, drag-to-reorder that persists, and transparency settings that stick. Command+, opens preferences. Auto-paste and Notes app compatibility refined.
